Get your licence.

KörkortMe is the practice app for the Swedish B licence theory test. Short lessons, real traffic situations, every road sign and realistic practice tests, with a source for every rule. In Swedish and English.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

  • The licence journeyLesson by lesson, from the basics to the final test.
  • Every road signThe full Swedish sign ordinance with pictures and explanations.
  • Practice tests70 questions in 50 minutes, like Trafikverket's test.
  • No accountYour progress stays on your phone.

KörkortMe is an independent practice app and is not affiliated with Trafikverket or Transportstyrelsen.
