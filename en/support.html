Support

Need help with KörkortMe? E-mail info@smartevo.nu and we'll reply as soon as we can, usually within two working days.

Common questions

Found a mistake in a question?

Tap Report question below the question and pick what's wrong. We then see exactly which question it is and can fix it in the next version.

How do I get Premium back on a new phone?

Open Premium in the app and tap Restore purchase. You need to be signed in with the same Google account (Android) or Apple ID (iPhone) you bought with. Purchases can't be moved between Android and iPhone. If it doesn't work, e-mail us the receipt and we'll help.

Does Premium renew automatically?

No. You buy a period (1 week, 1 month, 3 months or 12 months) and it simply ends. Nothing is charged unless you buy again yourself.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store: Google Play or App Store.

How do I delete my data?

Your progress is only on your phone. Choose More → Reset progress or delete the app. Read more in the privacy policy.

Is the app connected to Trafikverket?

No. KörkortMe is an independent practice app. You book the real theory test with Trafikverket.
